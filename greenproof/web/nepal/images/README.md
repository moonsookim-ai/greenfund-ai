# Satellite imagery credit and licence

These are unmodified JPEG images supplied by the European Space Agency (ESA).
The page displays the images at smaller sizes and offers a temporary comparison
mask in the browser. No image content, dates, labels, or colours have been edited.

The images show the Trishuli River corridor and the surroundings of Kathmandu.
They are not a map of district boundaries or an aid priority ranking. Cloud cover
obscures some areas. No household-level needs or support coverage are inferred
from these images.
